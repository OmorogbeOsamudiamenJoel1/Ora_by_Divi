Care Guide

A little care keeps your pieces shining for longer.

Gold-plated maintenance

Keep your jewelry dry and take it off before swimming, bathing or working out. Put it on after perfume, lotion and hair products. When you take it off, wipe it gently with a soft , dry cloth.
